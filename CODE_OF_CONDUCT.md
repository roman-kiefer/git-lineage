# Code of Conduct

Git Lineage is a small project maintained in spare time. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or reviews, is expected to keep it a place where people like to contribute.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, also when you disagree. Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent. Many contributors write in a language that is not their first one.
- Stay on topic and keep reports and reviews concrete: what happened, what you expected, what would help.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the project, and that "no" or "not now" is a legitimate answer.

## Not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, personal or political attacks, and discriminating remarks of any kind.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, and repeatedly pushing a topic after it was declined.
- Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces on GitHub (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, reviews) and wherever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behavior that breaks this code, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/roman-kiefer). Reports are treated confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently block people from the project. Usually a first, private notice comes before stronger steps; severe cases can lead to an immediate block.
